About

About me

I am a medical AI researcher with a background in computer science and artificial intelligence. My work covers medical image segmentation, disease classification and the analysis of clinical signals such as the electrocardiogram.

I am particularly interested in methods that clinicians can rely on: models that generalise across datasets, explain their decisions and report their own uncertainty. I am seeking a doctoral position where I can contribute to medical AI research with real clinical impact.

Current project · 2026

Lesion-level statistical guarantees for medical image segmentation

Segmentation models are usually judged by average scores, while clinical decisions are made lesion by lesion. This project applies conformal prediction to post-treatment brain metastasis segmentation to provide per-lesion guarantees on detection and missed findings.

    Explainable Atrial Fibrillation Detection from 12-Lead Electrocardiograms

    Atrial fibrillation classifier trained on PTB-XL and externally validated on Chapman-Shaoxing, with explanation methods that show which signal features drive each prediction.

    Code
  • 2026

    Multi-Label Knee MRI Abnormality Detection Using Report-Derived Weak Supervision

    Detection of 12 knee abnormalities on MRI without expert image labels. Soft labels were derived from multilingual radiology reports using a rule-based labeller, MedGemma and Qwen, and used to train a 2.5D ConvNeXt with per-finding attention. Achieved 0.880 macro AUC on the hidden test set.

    Code
  • 2026

    Rare-Class-Aware Multimodal Skin Lesion Diagnosis

    Multi-label classification of 11 skin lesion categories for the MILK10k / ISIC challenge, fusing dual-view dermatology images with patient and acquisition metadata. Handles severe class imbalance through rare-class-aware training and model ensembling.
